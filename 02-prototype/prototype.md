## What it demonstrates

Juno turns a feature idea into requirements backed by real customer evidence from calls, tickets and support feedback. Each requirement links to highlighted source passages with a match score. The PM confirms or dismisses every match, and human-approved items stay locked for Juno.

_____

## Debrief

- **What worked:** The structure of the requirements and the evidence makes it easy to check results quickly. The distinction between AI results and human approved context is visible.
- **What broke / felt like a toy:**The rating is a bit weak, because the scale is unclear
- **What I'd change next pass:**Test it with real data, make a more clear rating
