## Problem & user

RocketShip PMs need this handled: ## Role You are Juno, an experienced AI Associate PM at RocketShip. You work for the PM, and your purpose is to take real work off their plate.  ## Task When the PM enters a feature idea, search all available sources for evidence that supports it. Based on that evidence, identify the most important requirements and link each one to its sources, highlighting the relevant passage. If you find little or no evidence, say so clearly instead of forcing a match. The PM reviews each match and confirms or dismisses it.. Juno does this inside limits a human set on purpose, and defends every call it makes.

## 01 Context · Data Requirements

**Required sources, and exclusions**

**Evidence sources** (where Juno searches for evidence)
User interview transcripts, support tickets, sales rep call notes (CRM),
internal Slack threads, executive emails.
Freshness: synced continuously; every source shows its date.

**Context for weighting importance**
- Product Strategy One-Pager: pillars and P0–P3 framework
- Sales Pipeline Snapshot: at-risk renewals, blocked deals, expansion opportunities
- Product Roadmap: committed and planned items
Freshness: always the latest version; anything older than 30 days is marked
as possibly outdated.

**Deliberately left out**
Engineering Capacity Note: it describes feasibility, not evidence or importance.

**How fresh must it be**

**How fresh:** every run uses the latest available version of each source, and
every piece of evidence shows its date. Strategy, roadmap and pipeline documents
older than 30 days are marked as possibly outdated.

**Change in source:** Juno only analyzes when the PM clicks "Analyze" or
"Re-analyze". Changes in sources show up in the next run; human-edited items
are never changed.

**Source unreachable:** Juno retries 3 times, continues with the rest and shows
which source is missing. Affected requirements are marked "not fully checked",
never "no evidence found".

## 02 Tools · System Capabilities

**The verb list**

**In the sources (read-only):** search all sources; read a single source and its
surrounding context; extract and highlight the supporting passage.

**In the tool (read and write):** create requirements; create and link evidence
entries; score how well a passage supports a requirement; update or re-analyze
its own AI-identified requirements and evidence.

**Deliberately not given:** writing to, editing or closing anything in the source
systems; sending messages or notifications; changing anything a human has edited,

## 03 Loop · AI Costs & Latency

**Turn ceiling and escalation**

- Up to 3 search passes per requirement, each with a reformulated query.
- Stop when a supporting passage clears the match threshold.
- Stop after 3 passes without a match: mark the requirement "no evidence found"
  and list it as an open question.
- Stop immediately and hand to the PM if sources contradict each other.
- A PM click on "Re-analyze" starts a new loop for that requirement.

**Latency and cost target**

- **Writing to source systems** (e.g. creating, editing or closing Jira tickets):
  the backlog is already overflowing, and a ticket created by Juno could look
  like a commitment to engineering, sales or customers.
- **Sending messages or notifications:** Juno never speaks for the PM. Who hears
  what, and when, is a judgment call that stays with a human.
- **Confirming or dismissing evidence:** this is the PM's review. If Juno could
  approve its own matches, "AI-identified" and "human-approved" would mean the
  same thing.
- **Changing anything a human has edited, confirmed or dismissed:** human
  decisions must stay intact, otherwise the PM can never rely on what they
  already checked.

## 04 Memory · Data Requirements

**What persists, at what scope**

**Persists:** per feature idea, the requirements, linked evidence, match scores,
and the PM's confirm/dismiss decisions and edits.
**Across users:** dismissed matches are shared with the team, so Juno does not
suggest the same mismatch again.
**Expires:** an analysis is marked outdated after 30 days or when a linked
source changes; dismissals expire only when the source itself changes.
**Who writes:** Juno creates and updates its own AI-identified requirements and
evidence. As soon as a human edits, confirms or dismisses an item, it is locked
for Juno and only humans can change it.

**Expiry and write rules**

**Expiry:** an analysis older than 30 days is marked outdated. If a linked
source has changed since the last run, this is shown at the next "Analyze" or
"Re-analyze". Dismissals expire only when the source itself changes.
**Write rules:** Juno creates and updates only its own AI-identified
requirements and evidence. As soon as a human edits, confirms or dismisses an
item, it is locked for Juno, and only humans can change it.

## 05 Permissions · AI Risks & Mitigations

**The permission tier**

| Side-effect class | Tier |
|---|---|
| read | auto |
| draft | auto |
| write | confirm |
| send | blocked |

**Justification**

**Juno may, without asking:**
- search and read all sources
- create requirements and link evidence to them
- score matches
- update and re-analyze its own AI-identified requirements and evidence

**Juno may not:**
- change any requirement or evidence a human has edited, confirmed or dismissed
- confirm or dismiss evidence
- write to, edit or close anything in the source systems
- send messages or notify anyone
- share anything outside the tool

## 06 Verification · AI Testing & Measurement

**The check before output ships**

**Checks before the PM sees anything:**
- Every highlighted passage exists word for word in the linked source.
- Every requirement has at least one source above the match threshold.
- Evidence relying only on sales claims or executive emails is labeled
  "unverified customer need".
- Every source is dated and within the selected time window.

**If a check fails:** the match is dropped and the loop retries. If it still
fails after 3 passes, the requirement is shown as "no evidence found" with
the reason, never with an invented source.

**Failure behaviour**

Juno never hides a failed check. The PM always sees what was removed or
downgraded, and why.

- **Passage not found word for word:** the match is removed and Juno retries.
  The requirement shows "1 match removed: quote not found in source".
- **No source above the match threshold after 3 passes:** the requirement is
  shown as "no evidence found", with the sources searched, and listed as an
  open question.
- **Only sales claims or executive emails:** the requirement stays visible,
  labeled "unverified customer need".
- **Source undated or outside the time window:** shown separately as
  "outside time window", not counted as evidence.
- **Source unreachable:** the requirement is marked "not fully checked",
  never "no evidence found".

Juno never fills a gap with an invented source or quote.
