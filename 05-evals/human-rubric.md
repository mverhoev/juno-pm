## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Juno’s evidence search for a feature idea
- **Value proposition:** Turn a feature idea into the most important requirements, each backed by real customer evidence I can check in one click.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sources fidelity (deal-breaker) | Does every linked source exist, and does every highlighted quote appear word for word in it? | Yes / No | _ | n/a | _ |
| Relevance | Does each linked passage actually support the requirement it is attached to? | 1 to 5 | Most passages only share keywords with the requirement but describe a different problem. | Most passages support the requirement; one or two are weak or tangential. | Every passage directly describes the customer need behind the requirement. |
| Honesty about gaps (deal-breaker) | When evidence is thin or missing, does Juno say so clearly instead of forcing a match? | Yes / No | _ | n/a | _ |
| Usefulness | Are these the most important requirements for the idea, specific enough to act on? | 1 to 5 | Generic requirements that just restate the feature idea. | Right topics, but one important requirement is missing or vague. | Distinct, specific requirements the PM would keep without edits. |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** 10 answers a week, always including the "no evidence found" results and the ones the PM corrected most.
- **Who scores them with you:** Me, plus one other PM every two weeks.
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** If our scores differ by 2 or more, or we disagree on a yes/no question, we talk it through and rewrite that description so it’s clearer next time. A disputed deal-breaker counts as a fail until we agree.

## Pass bar

An average of 4 or higher on Relevance and Usefulness, and every yes/no question passes.

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Sources fidelity: any "no"
- Honesty about gaps: any "no"
- Juno changes an item a human has edited, confirmed or dismissed, or reports “no evidence found” while a source was unreachable.
